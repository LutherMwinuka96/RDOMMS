 Frequently Asked Questions

          

            

              How do I create a new project?
              
            

            

              Go to Projects > Create Project from the sidebar menu. Fill in all required fields including project name,
              description, budget, and dates. Click "Submit Project" when done.
            

          

          

            

              How do I track project progress?
              
            

            

              Navigate to Projects > Status Tracking. You can view all projects in the Kanban board or table view. Click
              on any project to update its progress percentage.
            

          

          

            

              How do I change my password?
              
            

            

              Click on your profile picture in the top right corner, select "Change Password" from the dropdown menu.
              Enter your current password and new password, then click "Update Password".
            

          

          

            

              What browsers are supported?
              
            

            

              RDO|MMS works best on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Internet Explorer is not
              supported.
